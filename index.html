Once every craftsperson worked alone. Forge & Fable was built so no one has to.

Part guild hall, part story circle — this team is where survival crafters, RPG loremasters, horror screamers and cozy-game chefs share a hearth. Below are the two ideas we build every stream around.

The Forge

The craft. The grind. The late-night stream when the mic finally sounds right. Members here show up for each other, raid trains, co-op chaos, and the unglamorous work of building a channel from nothing — together it gets hammered into something real.

The Fable

The worlds we vanish into and the ones we tell about ourselves. Every member below wrote their own introduction — dogs and kids, deployments and diagnoses, first consoles and favourite classes. Read a few. You'll want to stay for the streams.
